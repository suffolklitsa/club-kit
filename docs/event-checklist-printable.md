# Event Checklist (Printable)

Planning checklist for Suffolk Law student org events (hybrid panel, reception, CLE). Confirm procedures with named offices.

## Six or more weeks out
- [ ] **Book room:** Reserve via My Suffolk portal student org room request (popular rooms go early).
- [ ] **Date, time & format:** Confirm date/time for evening students; choose format (in-person or hybrid).
- [ ] **Speakers & moderator:** Send date, location, format, and topics to panelists and moderator.
- [ ] **Co-hosts:** Contact co-hosting student orgs early to share planning and promotion.
- [ ] **Budget & sponsors:** Route food, drinks, and gifts via SBA / Dean of Students; collect invoice, ACH form, and W-9 for sponsors.

## Four weeks out
- [ ] **RSVP & short link:** Create RSVP form and short redirect link ([guide](rsvp.md)).
- [ ] **Flyer & QR code:** Generate QR code ([tool](../qr)) and distribute event flyers.
- [ ] **Speaker holds:** Send calendar holds to speakers with evening schedule.
- [ ] **Promotion:** Post on school calendar; request Professional & Career Development listing.

## Two to three weeks out
- [ ] **Student CLE credit:** Submit to Assoc. Dean for Professional & Career Development **at least 2 weeks before** event (live, at least 60 min; include event name, sponsors, CLE coordinator, date/time, format, panel, qualification info).
- [ ] **Audience questions:** Collect questions from members/co-hosts; draft short topic list for speakers.
- [ ] **Zoom & AV:** Book room mics, camera, and Zoom link with IT / media services.
- [ ] **Speaker prep call:** Hold 15-min briefing on logistics, schedule, and topics; record for absentees.

## One week out
- [ ] **Room layout:** Meet with Dean of Students office to confirm setup and floor plan.
- [ ] **Alcohol permit:** Submit alcohol event registration form if serving drinks at reception.
- [ ] **Media services:** Book photographer or event recording with media services.
- [ ] **Run of show:** Finalize schedule, speaking order, introductions, and thank-yous.
- [ ] **Speaker logistics:** Send final topic list, arrival details, and room instructions to speakers.

## Day of
- [ ] **Sign-in table:** Set up printed sign-in sheets and name tags.
- [ ] **CLE forms:** Have pre-filled, signed attendance sheets ready to distribute at the end.
- [ ] **Zoom operator:** Assign someone to run Zoom and monitor chat.
- [ ] **Photographer:** Assign someone to take event photos.

## After
- [ ] **Thank-yous:** Send thank-you notes to speakers and co-hosts.
- [ ] **CLE paperwork:** Submit completed attendance forms to the appropriate office.
- [ ] **Recap:** Post event photos and recap.
- [ ] **Roster:** Add new sign-ins to the organization member list.
